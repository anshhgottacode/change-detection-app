## 1. Method choices (as implemented in pipeline.py)

Two change-detection methods were evaluated: pixel-wise absolute difference (Diff) and the Structural Similarity Index (SSIM).

- Diff: absolute difference between the two grayscale images (cv2.absdiff).
- SSIM: skimage.metrics.structural_similarity, default 7x7 uniform window, data_range = 255. The change map is 1 - SSIM, scaled to 0-255.
- Resizing: the after-image is first resized to the exact size of the before-image (aspect ratio NOT preserved for this step). If the width is then above 1280 px, both images are scaled down proportionally to a width of 1280 px. (Correction: the earlier version of these notes said aspect ratio was preserved throughout; that was wrong.)
- Preprocessing: grayscale, then Gaussian blur (5x5 default; a kernel of 1 turns blur off).
- Thresholding: Otsu's method on the change map (automatic threshold).
- Post-processing: morphological opening then closing, 5x5 square kernel; contours with area >= 200 px are kept.
- Mask stages: raw = after thresholding; clean = after opening and closing; final = kept contours filled in. The reported change percentage and region count both come from the final mask.
- Heatmaps are min-max normalised per image pair, so colours are not comparable across pairs.
- Gaussian blur 5x5 was compared with no blur (kernel 1) using the Diff method.

## 3. What happened on the `nochange` control

First attempt (discarded): both methods returned 0.0% change, 0 regions and an Otsu threshold of 0. That pattern means the change map was all zeros, so the two files were most likely identical. It is not a valid false-positive test, so it is not used in the paper.

Retaken pair (two separate photos of an unchanged scene, processed size 648x1152). Measured:

| Method | Change % | Regions | Otsu threshold | Total time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diff (blur 5x5) | 0.759 | 10 | 26 | 18.7 ms |
| SSIM | 13.810 | 24 | 99 | 97.8 ms |

Diff blur 1 / blur 5, from fig5_stats.csv: raw 6.251 / 6.974, clean 0.588 / 1.290, final 0.337 / 0.759, regions 2 / 10.

Interpretation (not verified): on this single unchanged scene, SSIM produced about 18 times more false change than Diff, and morphology removed most of the raw speckle. This is one pair, so it is an observation, not evidence that SSIM is worse in general. Otsu always selects a threshold, so some change is flagged even when nothing changed.

## 6. Fig. 5 observations: effect of blur and morphology

Measured (Diff method, from fig5_stats.csv; percentages are changed pixels):

| Pair | Blur | Raw % | Clean % | Final % | Regions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nochange | 1 | 6.251 | 0.588 | 0.337 | 2 |
| nochange | 5 | 6.974 | 1.290 | 0.759 | 10 |
| pair1 | 1 | 1.774 | 1.650 | 1.764 | 5 |
| pair1 | 5 | 1.799 | 1.742 | 1.846 | 4 |
| pair2 | 1 | 32.633 | 16.533 | 15.506 | 150 |
| pair2 | 5 | 31.885 | 25.350 | 25.063 | 129 |
| pair3 | 1 | 31.612 | 28.426 | 29.056 | 36 |
| pair3 | 5 | 31.439 | 29.767 | 30.219 | 35 |

Measured observations:
- Opening and closing reduced the raw mask most on pair2 without blur (32.633% to 16.533%) and on the no-change pair without blur (6.251% to 0.588%). With a 5x5 blur the reduction was smaller (pair2: 31.885% to 25.350%).
- A 5x5 blur changed the raw percentage by less than 0.8 percentage points on every pair.
- A 5x5 blur did not lower the final percentage on any pair (nochange 0.337 to 0.759; pair1 1.764 to 1.846; pair2 15.506 to 25.063; pair3 29.056 to 30.219).
- The region count fell slightly on the three changed pairs (5 to 4, 150 to 129, 36 to 35) but rose on the no-change pair (2 to 10).
- The final mask can be lower or higher than the clean mask, because regions under 200 px are removed and the holes inside kept regions are filled (pair2 no blur: clean 16.533, final 15.506; pair1 blur 5: clean 1.742, final 1.846).
- Fig. 5 uses pair2 because it shows the effect most clearly. Mean and standard deviation across pairs are not reported, because the pairs are very different scenes.

Interpretation (not verified): the larger blurred mask on pair2 may come from nearby small regions merging into bigger blobs that opening no longer removes. TODO: check this in the pair2 blur-5 mask before writing it in the paper.

The percentages measure how much of the image is flagged, not whether the detection is correct. Accuracy needs ground-truth masks (Day 6).

## 7. Method comparison and timing

Measured (summary_day2.csv, default settings):

| Pair | Method | Change % | Regions | Otsu thr | Change-map ms | Total ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nochange | diff | 0.759 | 10 | 26 | 1.3 | 18.7 |
| nochange | ssim | 13.810 | 24 | 99 | 93.4 | 97.8 |
| pair1 | diff | 1.846 | 4 | 62 | 0.1 | 4.5 |
| pair1 | ssim | 3.406 | 4 | 102 | 121.4 | 125.8 |
| pair2 | diff | 25.063 | 129 | 61 | 0.5 | 25.9 |
| pair2 | ssim | 80.778 | 53 | 174 | 271.1 | 293.9 |
| pair3 | diff | 30.219 | 35 | 64 | 0.1 | 5.8 |
| pair3 | ssim | 56.368 | 4 | 112 | 111.8 | 116.6 |

Measured: SSIM reported a larger changed percentage than Diff on all four pairs. The SSIM change-map stage took 93-271 ms against 0.1-1.3 ms for Diff. These are single runs; repeat 10 times per setting and report mean and spread (Day 4 or Day 7).

Interpretation (not verified): SSIM is sensitive to local structure and texture, and its Otsu threshold sits on a different scale from the Diff threshold. Ground truth is needed before saying which method is more accurate.
