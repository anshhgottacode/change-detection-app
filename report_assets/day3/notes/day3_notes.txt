7. Anything odd
- Auto-exposure was not confirmed, so it cannot be stated definitively whether it was on or off.
- nochange increased from 0.759% to 17.044% after alignment, an unexpected increase in apparent change despite this being a no-change control.
- pair2 alignment failed because it did not produce enough good matches.

- shiftstatic improved substantially after alignment, while pair1 remained almost unchanged after normalisation.
Overall conclusion: Alignment significantly reduced false detections in shiftstatic while preserving the real-change signal in pair1. However, the satellite pair remains challenging, and the unexpected nochange result needs further investigation.
